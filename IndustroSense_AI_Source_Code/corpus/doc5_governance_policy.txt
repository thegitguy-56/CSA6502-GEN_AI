TITLE: Plant Digital Systems - AI-Assisted Diagnostics Governance Policy (Excerpt)

3.1 Human Oversight
Any diagnostic recommendation generated by an AI assistant that involves equipment shutdown, safety-critical intervention, or a confidence score below 0.6 must be routed to a certified maintenance engineer for review before action is taken. The AI system output must be clearly labelled "AI-Generated - Requires Human Review" in such cases.

3.2 Data Handling
Uploaded equipment images and voice notes are considered operational data and must not be retained beyond 30 days unless linked to an open incident record. Personally identifiable information (e.g., a technician's voice or face incidentally captured) must be redacted or excluded from any training or fine-tuning dataset.

3.3 Auditability
All prompts, retrieved source chunks, and generated responses must be logged with timestamps and equipment tag references to support later audit and incident investigation.

3.4 Prohibited Uses
The system shall not be used to generate outputs to bypass safety interlocks, falsify maintenance records, or provide guidance for equipment types outside its validated scope (rotating feedwater pump equipment only, as of this policy version).
