TITLE: Standard Operating Procedure - Rotating Equipment Bearing Inspection (SOP-MECH-014, Rev 3)

1. Purpose
This SOP defines the steps for periodic and fault-triggered inspection of rolling-element bearings on rotating equipment including pumps, motors, and fans.

2. Trigger Conditions
Perform an unscheduled inspection when any of the following occur:
- Vibration alarm (Zone C or D per ISO 10816) raised by condition monitoring system.
- Audible grinding, screeching, or rhythmic knocking from the bearing housing.
- Bearing housing temperature exceeds 80 degrees C or rises more than 15 degrees C above ambient baseline within one shift.
- Visible grease discoloration (dark brown/black) or metallic particles in used grease sample.

3. Inspection Steps
Step 1: Isolate and lock out equipment per LOTO-GEN-001.
Step 2: Remove bearing housing cover; photograph bearing races and rolling elements before cleaning.
Step 3: Inspect for spalling, pitting, discoloration (bluing indicates overheating), and brinelling (indentations from static overload).
Step 4: Measure radial and axial play with a dial gauge; compare to OEM tolerance (typically 0.01-0.04 mm for this bearing class).
Step 5: If any defect is found, tag the component as "DO NOT OPERATE" and escalate to the maintenance engineer for replacement scheduling.

4. Documentation
Log all findings, including photographs and vibration spectra, into the CMMS incident record with the equipment tag number and inspection date.

5. Related Fault Codes
Cross-reference with pump manual codes CP204-E11 and CP204-E12 for centrifugal pump bearing alarms.
